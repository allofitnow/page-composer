GALLERY FIT — THREE WAYS

NOW is the site as it stands: every slot is object-fit: cover against a fixed ratio, so anything wider than its slot loses the sides. A keeps the slot and letterboxes inside it — nothing is cropped, but the spare height is dead space, and at these ratios that is 113–179px above and below, against an 80px gap between rows. B gives the cell the image's own ratio instead: nothing cropped, nothing padded, the cell simply ends up shorter than its neighbours. Rows already mix heights by design — split 8·4 pairs a 16/9 with a bottom-aligned 1/1 — so a short cell is not foreign to the language. Images that already fit are untouched in all three. Real media, real ratios, straight off the CMS.
